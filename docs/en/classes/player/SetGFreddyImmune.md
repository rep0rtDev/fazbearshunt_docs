> <span class="fh-badge shared">shared</span> @Player@:SetGFreddyImmune(@boolean@ isImmune)

# Description

Immunity to Golden Freddy's ability (the Outworld).

# Arguments

> `1` @boolean@ **isImmune**

- Pass `true` to prevent Golden Freddy from taking the player into his dimension, or `false` to allow it.
