> <span class="fh-badge server">server</span> **highlight.Add(@Player@ ply, @table@<@Player@> victims, @number@ duration, @Color@ color = Color(255,0,0))**

# Description

Highlights all `victims` for the player `ply`, with configurable color and duration.

# Arguments

> `1` @Player@ **ply**

- The player who triggers the highlight.

> `2` @table@<@Player@> **victims**

- The players who will be highlighted.

> `3` @number@ **duration**

- How long the highlight lasts.

> `4` @Color@ **color** = Color(255,0,0)

- The highlight color.
